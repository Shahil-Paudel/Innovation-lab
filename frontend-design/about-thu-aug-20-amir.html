Our Story

Born in the Himalaya, Built on Trust

Sagarmatha Treks & Expeditions was founded in 2011 by Tenzing Lama, a Sherpa guide who grew up in the shadow of Everest. After spending over a decade working for larger trekking companies, Tenzing realized something was missing: the personal touch, the deep cultural connection, the genuine care for every trekker who walked through the door.

So he started his own company — not to be the biggest, but to be the best. To treat every client like family. To ensure that the porters and guides who make these journeys possible are paid fairly, treated with respect, and given the same opportunities as the trekkers they serve.

Today, Sagarmatha Treks is a team of over 30 local staff — guides, porters, kitchen crews, and office support — all from the regions we trek in. We know these mountains not just as destinations, but as home. And we invite you to experience them the way we do: with reverence, responsibility, and joy.
