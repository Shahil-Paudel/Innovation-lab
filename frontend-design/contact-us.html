Contact & Booking FAQs

Absolutely! We welcome you to visit our office in Thamel for a free consultation. No appointment necessary during office hours, though scheduling ahead ensures our full attention.

Our itineraries include proper acclimatization days. Our guides are trained to recognize altitude sickness symptoms and carry medical supplies. We also conduct twice-daily oximeter checks on high-altitude treks.

We recommend booking at least 2-3 months in advance for peak seasons (Spring and Autumn). For winter or monsoon treks, 1 month is usually sufficient. Last-minute bookings are sometimes possible depending on availability.

We accept bank transfers, credit cards (with a 3.5% processing fee), PayPal, and cash (USD, EUR, or NPR) upon arrival in Kathmandu. A 20% deposit confirms your booking.
