Connected to Everest via the South Col. Its south face rises 3.2 km in just 2.25 km of horizontal distance — one of the steepest and most formidable walls in the world.

Makalu

An isolated four-sided pyramid 19 km south-east of Everest. Its knife-edge ridges and steep pitches make it one of the most technically beautiful climbs in the Himalaya.

The "Turquoise Goddess" in Tibetan. With moderate slopes and straightforward routes, it is widely considered the most accessible 8,000-metre peak — a perfect first eight-thousander.

The "White Mountain" rises abruptly over 7,000 m from the Kali Gandaki valley. When first surveyed in 1808 it was believed to be the highest mountain in the world.

The "Mountain of the Spirit". The Manaslu Circuit, with its prayer flags and the high Larkya La pass, is now one of Nepal's most loved teahouse treks.

Named for its summit ridge, which stretches over 1.5 km long. It stands just 8 km from K2 and was first climbed in 1957 by a small Austrian team without oxygen.
