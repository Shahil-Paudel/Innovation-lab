It's August — monsoon. Rain-shadow Mustang & Dolpo are at their best right now.

About trekking in Nepal
8 of 14 eight-thousanders70 m → 8,848 m range870+ bird species650+ butterfly species

Nepal packs the planet's greatest altitude range into a country smaller than Portugal — subtropical jungle, mid-hill rhododendron forests and the highest mountains on Earth. Trekking here is a teahouse tradition: you walk village-to-village, eating dal bhat and sleeping under roofs, not tents.

Two windows dominate: spring (March–May) for bloom and autumn (September–November) for clarity. In the monsoon between, the rain-shadow deserts of Mustang and Dolpo stay dry — the secret season locals love.
