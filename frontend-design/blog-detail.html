Best Time to Trek in Nepal: A Month-by-Month Guide

Tenzing Lama
Aug 2, 2026 8 min read

Key Takeaways

  • Spring (Mar-May): Best for rhododendrons and clear mountain views.
  • Autumn (Sep-Nov): Most popular season with stable weather and festivals.
  • Winter (Dec-Feb): Excellent for lower altitude treks like Mardi Himal.

Choosing when to visit Nepal is the single most important decision you'll make for your trek. The Himalayas are majestic year-round, but the experience changes dramatically with the seasons. In this guide, we break down exactly what to expect month by month.

The Spring Season: March to May

Spring is one of the two peak seasons for trekking in Nepal. As the snow melts and the days grow longer, the hillsides explode with color. Rhododendrons, Nepal's national flower, paint the forests in vibrant reds, pinks, and whites.

Pro Tip

Book your teahouses early for April! It's the busiest month for Everest Base Camp trekkers.

The temperatures are moderate, making for comfortable walking days. However, afternoon clouds can sometimes obscure the highest peaks, so early morning starts are recommended for the best views.

Clear morning views of Annapurna South

The Autumn Season: September to November

Autumn is widely considered the absolute best time to trek in Nepal. Following the monsoon, the air is crisp, the skies are a piercing blue, and the visibility is unmatched.

Festivals and Culture

This season coincides with Nepal's biggest festivals: Dashain and Tihar. Trekking during this time offers a unique cultural immersion as you witness celebrations in remote villages.

  • Crystal clear mountain views every morning
  • Stable weather with minimal rainfall
  • Vibrant local festivals and culture
  • Busy trails — great for social trekkers

Winter & Summer: The Off-Peaks

While spring and autumn get the glory, winter and summer offer unique advantages for the right traveler.

  1. Winter (Dec-Feb): Cold at high altitudes, but perfect for lower treks like Poon Hill or Mardi Himal. You'll have the trails almost to yourself.
  2. Summer/Monsoon (Jun-Aug): Rainy and leeches in lower forests, but the rain-shadow regions like Upper Mustang and Dolpo are perfect and dry.

Final Thoughts

There is no "bad" time to trek in Nepal, only different experiences. If you want flowers and warmth, choose Spring. If you want crystal views and culture, choose Autumn. If you want solitude, choose Winter.
