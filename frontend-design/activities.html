Pick of the Month — August 2026

Upper Mustang MTB Expedition

10 Days Challenging Max 3,840 m ★ 4.9 (64)

The rain-shadow desert of Upper Mustang, by bike. Ride dirt roads and singletrack from Jomsom to the walled kingdom of Lo Manthang — cave monasteries, Tibetan Buddhist culture, wind-carved cliffs, and virtually no monsoon rain even in August.

Why this month?

Mustang sits in the Himalayan rain-shadow. While monsoon drowns the rest of Nepal, the desert kingdom stays dry and blue-skied — August is actually its secret season.

Before your trek

The shakeout day

Arrive a day early. See seven UNESCO sites with a licensed guide, calibrate to altitude and time zone, and meet your trek guide over a traditional Newari lunch.

Recovery day

Wheels down in Pokhara

After Annapurna, swap boots for a bike. Sunrise at Sarangkot, 1,200 m of beginner-friendly downhill, and lakeside lunch. The perfect decompression day.

Can't trek this year?

Everest, at breakfast

Knee injury, tight schedule, travelling with older parents — no reason to miss Everest. Fly there, land at Kalapatthar, breakfast at Everest View. Back by lunch.

About activities in Nepal
870+ bird species 125+ mammals 12 national parks 6 UNESCO sites 55,000 km of trails

Beyond trekking, Nepal packs an extraordinary density of experiences into a small country. Birders come for the 870+ species (including 40+ globally threatened ones); safari-goers for one-horned rhinos and Royal Bengal tigers; heli travellers for the chance to have breakfast at the highest hotel in the world, with Everest filling the window.

Our activity program is built by the same guides who lead our treks — which means we know which heli pilot has the smoothest flying record, which Chitwan naturalist actually spots tigers, and which Kathmandu heritage guide can tell you why the Kama Sutra was carved on a temple wall in Patan.
